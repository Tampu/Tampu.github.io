Questions I'm carrying

AThe universe and our place in it
  • Why is there something rather than nothing?
  • Is the universe fundamentally understandable to us?
  • Are mathematics and physics discoveries about reality, or frameworks we use to describe it?
  • Does understanding the cosmos make a single life feel smaller, or more meaningful?
BEvolution, beyond biology
  • Is evolution directional, or does it only look that way to us?
  • Are biological, cultural and technological evolution connected?
  • What would it mean for consciousness to evolve?
CAI and the future of being human
  • Does AI extend human intelligence, or substitute for it?
  • If we outsource memory and reasoning, do we slowly lose them?
  • Is intelligence the same thing as wisdom?
  • What should we keep learning for ourselves?
DWhat can we actually know?
  • Are there truths humans are not built to understand?
  • Is scientific knowledge an approximation of reality?
  • Can consciousness understand itself completely?
  • Is uncertainty a limit of intelligence, or a feature of reality?
