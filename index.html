Tampu Ravi Kumar

PhD student in Computer Science at Arizona State University

CoRAL Lab, ASU

Tempe, AZ

I am a PhD student in Computer Science at Arizona State University, advised by Prof. Vivek Gupta in the CoRAL lab. My research is on multimodal reasoning and evidence attribution and grounding across tables, images, documents and speech, and on RL post-training (GRPO) for LLMs and VLMs. I build methods that check whether a model’s answer is actually grounded in the evidence it cites.

Before my PhD, I spent three years as a Data Scientist at Phoenix Group in Edinburgh, UK, working on churn prediction, customer segmentation and speech analytics. I hold an MSc in Big Data Science (Distinction) from Queen Mary University of London and a BE in Electrical and Electronics Engineering from M. S. Ramaiah Institute of Technology, Bengaluru. During my second-year of MSc I was a KU Leuven International Scholar at imec, Belgium.

I am currently a Graduate Teaching Assistant for CSE 576 (NLP), CSE 475 (Foundations of ML) and CSE 511 (Data Processing at Scale).
